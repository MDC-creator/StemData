# Sample images (not in Git)

Put the sample `.czi` files here (from `StemCDataHub_Beispieldaten.zip`: `New-04.czi`, `New-05.czi`,
`New-09.czi`). They are research data of the partner lab and must not be committed or uploaded to GitHub.

`scripts/setup.sh` loads them as demo content on the first run. Without them the app still starts;
you can add images later on the Upload page.
